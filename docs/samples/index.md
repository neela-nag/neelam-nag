## Why I include context notes

A document without context tells you what someone wrote. A document with context tells you why — who the reader is, what they need to accomplish, and what structural decisions the writer made to serve that need.

The context notes on each sample are part of the sample. A senior technical writer is not just someone who writes clearly — they are someone who can explain why they structured content the way they did, what alternatives they considered, and what they would improve next. That thinking is what I am demonstrating here.
