## What I do

Documentation for **developer-facing platforms**. APIs, payment integrations, SDKs, and the knowledge systems holding them up.

<div class="nn-sample-grid" markdown>

<div class="nn-sample-card" markdown>
### Start with the reader

I usually write down what the reader is trying to get done, in their own words, before I touch the page structure. The hierarchy figures itself out from there. When it doesn't, that's a sign the product itself needs a conversation.
</div>

<div class="nn-sample-card" markdown>
### Structure over prose

A 250-page portal that no one can navigate is just 250 abandoned pages. So I spend a lot of time on the unglamorous parts: taxonomy, naming, where a thing lives, how it gets found.
</div>

<div class="nn-sample-card" markdown>
### What changed after we shipped
I want to know if support tickets dropped. If the integration team stopped pinging the same engineer at 6pm. If a page sits unread, I'd rather find out and fix it than congratulate myself for shipping.
</div>

## Philosophy

<div class="nn-philosophy">
  <p>Most of my work isn't writing. It's deciding what doesn't need to be on the page at all, and then putting the rest in the order someone will actually meet it. I start with the task the reader came in with, not the engineering team's mental model of the product. Five minutes from now, did they get unstuck? That's the test I keep coming back to, and most of my structural decisions come out of asking it honestly.</p>
</div>
